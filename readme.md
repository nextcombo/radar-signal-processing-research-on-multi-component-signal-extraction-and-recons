# Radar Signal Processing: Research on Multi-component Signal Extraction and Reconstruction Based on Time-Frequency Distribution with MATLAB Codes

Firstly, the common radar signal types' time-frequency characteristics and sparse features were summarized, and further research was conducted on the regularities present in multicomponent radar signals in their time-frequency distribution. The study found that when calculating time-frequency distributions, it is inevitable to balance between interference suppression and signal blurring. Therefore, an adaptive directional kernel-based second time-frequency distribution was introduced. This distribution achieves a relatively ideal balance between interference suppression and signal blurring by adaptively selecting directional kernels in the blurred domain based on the characteristics of the signal and interference items in this domain.
Subsequently, to investigate the component structure of signals, an algorithm for estimating instantaneous frequency was developed. It was found that existing algorithms were inadequate for signals with intersecting components, leading to the introduction of a gradient rotation method to enhance the time-frequency distribution image. A segmentation and fitting algorithm based on endpoint gradients was proposed, which not only eliminated errors in component instantaneous frequency tracking but also reduced estimation errors.
Finally, based on instantaneous frequency estimation, a time-varying filtering method is employed to extract and reconstruct the components' signals. Analysis reveals that significant distortion occurs at the intersections of the components, leading to the introduction of an amplitude correction algorithm and the proposal of a short-time fractional order Fourier transform with time-varying orders time-varying filtering algorithm. This proposed algorithm significantly enhances the accuracy of signal extraction and reconstruction, especially for nonlinear modulated frequency signals. In addressing the contradiction between cross-term interference and signal blurring in multi-component signal time-frequency analysis, an adaptive directional kernel time-frequency distribution algorithm has been introduced, yielding an ideal time-frequency distribution image. On this image, rotation gradient enhancement and instantaneous frequency connection fitting algorithms are used to extract the instantaneous frequencies of each component. Finally, a time-varying filtering algorithm based on time-varying orders of short-time fractional order Fourier transform is applied to extract and reconstruct the time-domain waveforms of each component. The process of signal extraction and reconstruction is decomposed into four steps: signal modeling, time-frequency analysis, instantaneous frequency extraction, and time-varying filtering, forming a comprehensive and effective scheme for multi-component radar signal extraction and reconstruction.
